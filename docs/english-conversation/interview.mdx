### 13. Could you share with us a challenging project you worked on recently? What was your role, and how did you overcome the challenges?

In a recent project, I was the Team Lead for a project that involved managing project history across multiple countries.
The project was complex because we had to integrate data from various regions while ensuring that all stakeholders' requirements were met.
The biggest challenge I faced was managing multiple tasks under tight deadlines and ensuring that the resources in my team were utilized effectively.
To overcome this, I prioritized tasks based on urgency and impact, delegated responsibilities clearly,
and maintained open communication with my team to address any issues promptly.
This approach helped us deliver the project on time and meet the client's expectations.

### 14. Can you provide more details on how you managed to balance multiple tasks and resources within your team? Were there any specific strategies or tools you used to ensure everything was completed on time?

To manage multiple tasks and resources, I relied on several key strategies.
First, I used a project management tool like Jira to track tasks, deadlines, and resource allocation.
This allowed me to have a clear overview of the project's progress and identify any potential bottlenecks early on.
I also implemented a daily stand-up meeting with my team to quickly address any issues and adjust priorities if needed.
Additionally, I encouraged a culture of transparency and collaboration, which helped ensure that everyone was on the same page.
By staying organized and fostering open communication, we were able to complete the project successfully and on time.
